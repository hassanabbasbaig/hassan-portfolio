Approach

Start with the economic question, then choose the method.

My toolkit ranges from semi-structural macro models and panel econometrics to machine learning, geospatial analysis and knowledge graphs. The method changes with the problem.

01. FrameTranslate the economic or policy question into testable assumptions and measurable outcomes.
02. BuildConstruct consistent data, indicators and model inputs from structured, granular or unstructured sources.
03. EstimateUse forecasting, econometrics, machine learning or scenario analysis appropriate to the question.
04. InterpretConnect quantitative results back to economic mechanisms, uncertainty and decision implications.
