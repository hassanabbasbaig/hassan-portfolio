Research

Working papers, applied research and current research infrastructure.

Research infrastructure

Multilingual knowledge discovery at scale

Built a reusable platform over 2.8+ million articles using Neo4j, Qdrant, embeddings, multilingual NLP and local LLM/GraphRAG workflows.

Data integration

1.4M+ harmonised survey observations

Harmonised individual- and household-level observations and linked news, survey, event, climate and satellite sources into spatially and temporally aligned measures for microeconometric analysis.

Methods

Quantitative methods across macro, micro and data science.

Tools are chosen around the economic question: econometrics and semi-structural models where interpretation matters, machine learning where prediction or dimensionality demands it, and data engineering where the evidence itself must first be made usable.
