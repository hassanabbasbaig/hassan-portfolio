Research & methods

Econometric questions supported by reproducible data systems.

My work spans macroeconomic forecasting and monetary transmission as well as current microeconometric research combining multilingual text, survey data, events, geospatial evidence, climate and satellite sources.

Master’s thesis · 2026–Present

Long-term institutional change following natural disasters

Investigating institutional change by combining 25 years of multilingual news with 26 years of household and individual survey data, structured events and geospatial evidence.

Research infrastructure

Multilingual knowledge discovery at scale

Built a reusable platform over 2.8+ million articles using Neo4j, Qdrant, embeddings, multilingual NLP and local LLM/GraphRAG workflows.

Data integration

1.4M+ harmonised survey observations

Harmonised individual- and household-level observations and linked news, survey, event, climate and satellite sources into spatially and temporally aligned measures for later microeconometric analysis.
