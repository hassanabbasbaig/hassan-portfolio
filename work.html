Selected work

From macroeconomic scenarios to granular-data econometrics.

A selection of projects documented in my CV, spanning forecasting, policy transmission, survey infrastructure, machine learning and large-scale research systems.

Case 01

Domain
Macroeconomic policy
Methods
QPM/IRIS, SVAR, forecasting, scenarios
Setting
Central bank

Medium-Term Macroeconomic Framework

Analytical task

Build a consistent medium-term view of the economy rather than treating real, fiscal, external, monetary and financial-sector forecasts as separate exercises.

Contribution

I prepared and integrated sectoral forecasts into baseline and alternative scenarios for medium-term policy assessment. The work combined QPM/IRIS, SVARs, econometric forecasting, MATLAB-based analysis and supervisory datasets to assess inflation, output, financial conditions and transmission of policy and external shocks.

Application

The framework was used to evaluate structural reforms, sovereign-financing developments, financial regulation, geopolitical shocks and other medium-term policy questions within a common quantitative setting.

Case 02

Domain
Monetary transmission
Methods
Panel/cohort fixed effects, time effects, QGIS
Data
eCIB supervisory lending records

Heterogeneous bank-lending responses to monetary-policy shocks

Research question

How do lending responses differ across institutions, borrowers and monetary-policy cycles?

Contribution

I estimated heterogeneous bank-lending responses across easing and tightening cycles using 42 months of eCIB data covering 72 institutions, roughly 3 million borrowers and roughly 5 million loans per month. The empirical work used panel/cohort fixed-effects and time-effects models, complemented by QGIS spatial analysis.

Research value

The project demonstrates work with very large supervisory microdata and connects monetary-policy questions to borrower- and institution-level evidence.

Case 03

Domain
Expectations measurement
Methods
Survey design, sampling, implementation
Setting
Central bank + academic partner

SBP–IBA Survey Research Centre

Measurement need

The central bank required systematic, recurring evidence on consumer expectations rather than one-off measurement exercises.

Contribution

As SBP focal point and technical lead, I helped establish the Survey Research Centre and took the Consumer Confidence Survey from research and sampling design through piloting and implementation into a permanent nationwide platform. I designed survey methodology and sampling, cleaned and geographically mapped 3.5 million records, and translated methodology into operational and system requirements for CRM, telephony, data capture, monitoring and reporting.

Institutional value

The work combined economic measurement, large-scale data preparation and implementation with developers and vendors to create a recurring research platform.

Case 04

Domain
Forecasting
Methods
LASSO, SVM, clustering
Tools
Python, MATLAB

High-frequency forecasting and machine-learning extensions

Forecasting problem

Conventional macroeconomic data arrive with lags, while policy analysis often requires faster signals from granular and high-frequency indicators.

Contribution

I built high-frequency GDP nowcasting workflows using LASSO and applied SVM and clustering methods to inflation, trade-in-services and workers’ remittance forecasting. I also linked firm financial statements, daily tax collections, business-level FDI and weekly credit flows to real, fiscal and external-sector forecasts.

Analytical value

The work extended standard forecasting with higher-frequency evidence and machine-learning methods while retaining an economic forecasting framework.

Case 05

Domain
Master’s thesis research
Methods
NLP, Knowledge Graphs, GraphRAG
Scale
2.8M+ multilingual articles

Multilingual knowledge-discovery platform

Research need

Long-run institutional research requires evidence spread across millions of multilingual news articles and multiple structured and geospatial datasets.

Contribution

I built a reusable knowledge-discovery platform over more than 2.8 million news articles spanning 25 years, using Neo4j, Qdrant, embeddings, multilingual NLP and local LLM/GraphRAG workflows.

Research value

The platform provides persistent, connected retrieval infrastructure for identifying and linking evidence across a long historical corpus.

Case 06

Domain
Long-run empirical research
Methods
Harmonisation, spatial/temporal linkage
Scale
1.4M+ survey observations

Survey, event, climate and satellite data integration

Research problem

Studying long-term institutional change after natural disasters requires sources with different structures, geographic units and time scales to be made analytically comparable.

Contribution

I harmonised more than 1.4 million individual- and household-level observations and linked news, survey, event, climate and satellite sources to construct spatially and temporally aligned measures for subsequent microeconometric analysis.

Research value

The resulting infrastructure connects long-run household evidence to events and environmental exposure in a reproducible empirical workflow.
