Selected work

From analytical gaps to durable capability.

A selection of projects that show how I approach institutional problems: define the decision need, identify the structural information gap, and build repeatable analytical systems around it.

Case 01

Domain
Monetary policy
Methods
Forecasting, scenario analysis, simulation
Setting
Central bank

Macroeconomic Framework & Forecasting Unit

Decision problem

Monetary-policy decisions require coherent, regularly updated projections and a disciplined view of risks rather than disconnected model outputs.

Contribution

I led a three-analyst unit responsible for recurring forecast and risk updates, and worked on forecasting models, policy-simulation tools, analytical data infrastructure, standardised outputs and briefing material supporting monetary-policy processes.

Institutional value

The emphasis was not one forecast, but a repeatable production system linking data, models, scenarios, team workflows and executive communication.

Case 02

Domain
Expectations & monetary policy
Methods
Survey design, governance, analysis
Setting
Central bank

Inflation expectations & survey research capability

Decision problem

Expectations matter for monetary policy, but are difficult to observe systematically without an enduring measurement process.

Contribution

My work included the implementation of an inflation-expectations measurement framework and the development of survey capability for recurring household and business evidence. This involved methodology, governance, software/process design and analytical integration.

Institutional value

The objective was to move from occasional observation toward structured, recurring evidence that could support policy analysis over time.

Case 03

Domain
Economic strategy
Methods
Structural modelling, scenarios
Output
Four medium-term frameworks

Medium-term macroeconomic policy frameworks

Decision problem

Strategic reforms and macroeconomic risks are often expressed qualitatively, making economy-wide trade-offs difficult to compare consistently.

Contribution

I co-developed four medium-term macroeconomic policy frameworks that translated reform assumptions into quantified scenarios through structural modelling, policy simulation and economy-wide impact assessment.

Institutional value

The frameworks created a common quantitative language for comparing strategic policy choices and their medium-term implications.

Case 04

Domain
Applied microeconomics
Methods
Causal inference, geospatial analysis
Setting
University research

Administrative records, geography & weather

Research problem

Causal analysis of administrative-court outcomes required information trapped in thousands of PDF records and disconnected from spatial and weather data.

Contribution

I built reproducible workflows for document extraction and standardisation, geospatial integration, weather matching and analysis-ready data construction before estimation.

Research value

The pipeline turned fragmented source material into reusable causal-research infrastructure rather than a one-off manually assembled dataset.

Case 05

Domain
Household surveys
Methods
Python, ETL, harmonisation
Focus
Reproducibility

Multi-decade household-survey harmonisation

Research problem

Successive household-survey waves are difficult to use longitudinally when variable names, structures and coding conventions change over time.

Contribution

I designed a Python-based harmonisation system to standardise and integrate survey waves across multiple decades, with reusable transformations rather than ad-hoc cleaning.

Research value

The project treats data preparation as research infrastructure: explicit, repeatable and extensible as new waves or variables are added.

Case 06

Domain
Knowledge systems
Methods
NLP, vectors, graph databases
Status
Exploratory

Multilingual knowledge & retrieval systems

Information problem

Large multilingual information collections become difficult to search, connect and reason over when retrieval is limited to documents or keywords.

Exploration

I have been exploring automated ingestion, entity extraction, vector retrieval, graph databases and local language-model interfaces as components of reusable knowledge infrastructure.

Why it matters

This work extends the same institutional pattern into newer technology: build a system that makes fragmented evidence easier to retrieve and connect for repeated analytical use.
